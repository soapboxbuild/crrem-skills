Assess the CRREM stranding risk for this building. Using the CRREM v2.05 pathways data:

1. Fetch the pathway targets for the building's country and property type
2. Compare the current carbon intensity (kgCO₂/m²yr) against the CRREM pathway
3. Calculate the stranding year — when the building crosses above the pathway
4. Show a table of current vs. target intensity by year (2025, 2030, 2035, 2040, 2050)
5. Quantify the performance gap and what reduction is needed to stay on-pathway
