---
description: Show CRREM v2.05 carbon and energy pathway targets
argument-hint: [country] [property type] e.g. "US office" or "DE residential"
---

Show the CRREM v2.05 decarbonization pathway targets for the specified country and property type.

Fetch the pathway data from `soapboxbuild/crrem-data` and display:
- Carbon intensity targets (kgCO₂/m²yr) by year from 2025 to 2050
- Energy intensity targets (kWh/m²yr) by year from 2025 to 2050
- The implied annual reduction rate required
